Pay publicly

Public to public, on the public-balance service.

Shield

Public to private: a deferred transfer carries the note commitment.

The Empire sees your address, the amount and a note commitment. Not who will own the note.

Send privately

Private to private: proved in this browser, verified in refine.

The Empire sees two nullifiers, two note commitments and two sealed notes. Not you, not the recipient, not the amount.

Unshield

Private to public: the proof names the account, accumulate pays it.

The Empire sees the amount and your address. Not which notes paid for it.
